# **🐦‍🔥My Top 1: Harry Potter**

> *-It is our choices, Harry, that show what we truly are, far more than our abilities.*

*—Albus Dumbledore.*

**Description:** Written by J.K. Rowling, the Harry Potter book series follows a young orphan named Harry Potter who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is a wizard. He attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he forms deep friendships with Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger while mastering magic. Across the seven-part saga, Harry and his allies lead the resistance against Lord Voldemort, a dark wizard bent on achieving immortality and conquering the wizarding world.

# ✨🪄My Favorite Characters
• **Harry Potter** – The main hero and the "Boy Who Lived" who faces Lord Voldemort.  

• **Hermione Granger** – Harry's brilliant, loyal, and resourceful best friend.

# **🔥My Top 2: Percy Jackson**

<img width="740" height="415" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c2d16990-b700-4f71-a5bd-550bbf6d6112" />

> *-Forget the oracle! You're worried about your destiny, then write a new one.*
 
*—Annabeth Chase.*

**Description:** Written by Rick Riordan, the fantasy book series Percy Jackson & the Olympians follows Percy Jackson, a young boy who discovers he is the demigod son of Poseidon. Along with his friends at Camp Half-Blood, he trains his abilities and navigates a modern world packed with ancient Greek gods and monsters. Together, they must complete high-stakes quests to stop the evil Titan Kronos from destroying Western civilization.

# ✨🪄My Favorite Characters
• **Percy Jackson**: The main protagonist, a demigod who is the son of Poseidon.  

• **Annabeth Chase**: A brilliant demigod daughter of Athena, known for her sharp intelligence, tactical mind, and dream of becoming an architect.

• **Grover Underwood**: Percy’s loyal satyr best friend who acts as his protector and searches for the god Pan.

• **Nico di Angelo**: The powerful demigod son of Hades who can control the dead and shadows

>*I'm the man who's going to be the King of the Pirates. I don't have time to be afraid.”*
>
*— Monkey D. Luffy*

**Description:** Netflix's One Piece live-action series is an action-adventure adaptation of Eiichiro Oda's iconic manga. The story follows young, rubber-bodied pirate Monkey D. Luffy as he sets out on a perilous ocean voyage to find the legendary treasure known as the "One Piece" and become King of the Pirates. Along the way, he gathers a ragtag crew—known as the Straw Hat Pirates—comprising Roronoa Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Sanji, as they battle rival crews and navigate the dangerous Grand Line.
